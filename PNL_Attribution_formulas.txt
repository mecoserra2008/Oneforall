-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THESE ARE WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three separate causes, none of them typos:

  1. THE SWAPS SHEET GAINED TEN COLUMNS.
     "DV01 BBG" plus a nine-column BQL block were added at O:X, which pushed
     everything from OIS_T0 onward out to Y:BZ. Eight formulas here still point
     at where those columns used to be:

         Swaps!$BN$  ->  Swaps!$O$     was DV01_BBG, is now FloatCurve_T0
         Swaps!$AG$  ->  Swaps!$AQ$    was Swap_ID_Source, is now PV_T-1 anuidade
         Swaps!$AB$  ->  Swaps!$AL$    was PnL, is now DF_T-1

     So every swap SUMIFS is summing a curve level as if it were a risk number,
     and every PLAIN/SYNTHETIC filter is testing an annuity against the word
     "PLAIN" and matching nothing. Every swap hedge currently reads as ZERO.

  2. THREE BONDS REFERENCES ARE ONE COLUMN TOO FAR RIGHT.
     Bonds runs A:CL, 90 columns. BP is Bond_Status, BQ is SwapLink, CL is
     DV01_Opening_EUR.

         Bonds!BQ4   ->  Bonds!BP4     read SwapLink, wanted Bond_Status
         Bonds!CM4   ->  Bonds!CL4     CM is PAST THE END of the sheet

     The second is the expensive one. Bond_DV01_Opening (CE) is blank on every
     row, so PnL_OIS, PnL_GovBasis, PnL_Credit_Ispread, PnL_ZSpread, PnL_GSpread
     and the whole PnL_Duration_Total chain are all zero or blank.

  3. TWO LIVE #REF! ERRORS, in BG and CC, plus an inverted ratio in BG.

The FUTURES references are already correct - AB, J, AM, AO and U all match the
sheet. Nothing in the futures chain needs a formula change; it was the loader
writing to the wrong columns, and that is fixed in the macro.

===============================================================================
THE #REF! AND THE INVERTED RATIO
===============================================================================

BG  Hedge_Efficiency
    Was: IF(AND(ISNUMBER(#REF!);ISNUMBER(T5);T5<>0);1-ABS(T5)/ABS(SUM(O5:Q5));"")
    Two faults. The #REF! was N5, the actual hedge DV01. And the ratio was
    upside down - it measured the TARGET against the hedge, so a bond with a
    tiny hedge scored well. Efficiency is how close the hedge came to the
    target: 1 when they match, falling away in either direction.
=IF(AND(ISNUMBER(N5);ISNUMBER(T5);T5<>0);1-ABS(N5-T5)/ABS(T5);"")

===============================================================================
TWO MORE WORTH FIXING WHILE YOU ARE IN THERE
===============================================================================

AO  Funding_Carry_Memo
    It averages Bonds!BO4 with Bonds!BP4 as if they were FundRate_T0 and
    FundRate_T-1. There is no FundRate_T-1 column - BP is Bond_Status, so the
    average never applies and the AVERAGE branch is dead code that happens to
    fall through to the right answer. Simplified to the single rate that
    actually exists.
=IF(AND(ISNUMBER(Bonds!AH4);ISNUMBER(K5));LET(_fr;Bonds!BO4;IF(ISNUMBER(_fr);-Bonds!AH4*IF(ABS(_fr)>1;_fr/100;_fr)*K5;""));"")

BB  Official_Total_PnL
    N(CG5) returns 0 for text but PROPAGATES an error, so one bad coupon cell
    takes the whole official PnL - and then Total_Model_Explained, the residual
    and the row's place in the headline - with it. IFERROR(x*1;0) maps both a
    blank and an error to zero.
=IF(AND(ISNUMBER(U5);ISNUMBER(BW5));U5+IFERROR(CG5*1;0)+BW5;"")
